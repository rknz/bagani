BAGANI (বাগানি) — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026
Developer: ProFix - Web & Soft

1. Introduction

BAGANI is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data when you use the BAGANI mobile application and related web services.

2. Data We Collect & Why

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used strictly upon your explicit action to scan QR tags on your rooftop plant pots and capture leaf photos for botanical identification and disease pathology diagnosis. Photos are not used for advertising or profiling.
  • Coarse Location (Approximate District): Used solely to provide localized weather forecasts, humidity data, and weather-aware watering guidance. We do not access precise GPS or background location.
  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we process your username, email, and preferred district. Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed; we never store plain text passwords.

3. Cloud Synchronization & Data Storage

Your garden records (plant tags, care schedules, timeline logs) are stored locally on your device and can be securely backed up to your connected cloud account or exported as an offline JSON backup.

4. Third-Party Services

BAGANI connects with verified botanical encyclopedias (Wikipedia REST API) and AI Vision gateways (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision) solely for botanical analysis.

5. In-App & Web Account Deletion (Apple 5.1.1 & Google Play)

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete your account and all associated garden records at any time directly inside the app (Profile > Delete Account) or online via our Account Deletion Request Page.
