---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

The safety and privacy of urban gardeners using BAGANI is of paramount importance.

If you discover a security vulnerability or privacy concern:

1. **Do not publicly disclose the issue.**
2. Send an email with full details, reproduction steps, and potential impact to:  
   📧 **security@profixwebsoft.com** (or contact via GitHub security advisory).
3. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and work with you on a swift, coordinated patch.

---

## Google Play Security Standards

BAGANI adheres strictly to:
- No background location tracking.
- No broad storage permissions (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`).
- Encrypted HTTPS-only communications for meteorological and botanical lookups.
